## What's New in v4.3

* **Interactive Onboarding Screen:** Added a beautiful setup screen for first-time app launches to smoothly configure essential permissions.
* **Smart Permissions Flow:** Tapping "Continue Setup" now intelligently prompts you for missing permissions sequentially, rather than bombarding you.
* **Polished Iconography:** The onboarding screen now proudly displays the true circular Gabi app icon.
* **Adaptive Icon Crash Fix:** Resolved a crash caused by the system struggling to render Android's adaptive XML app icon format by using Coil to cleanly rasterize it.
* **Squishy Physics Refined:** The floating pill's action buttons (like the Instant Download and Clear History buttons) now bounce and squash into existence cleanly from a small dot, rather than awkwardly clipping their width.
